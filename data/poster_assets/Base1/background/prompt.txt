Create a fresh cohesive background artwork for a portrait 3-by-3 poster layout.

Primary requirement:
- The background must be created around the poster composition from the start,
  not repaired after the fact.
- Background only, no characters and no creatures.
- Use `composition_guide.png` as a strict spatial planning reference. Its three
  visible Pokemon show the exact identities, silhouettes, sizes, and positions
  that the renderer will composite later. Do not draw them in the background.
- The blue and cyan rectangles are reserved title and info regions. The faint
  grid and yellow baseline are guides only and must not appear in the artwork.
- The pink halos around the Pokemon are foreground-detail exclusion zones.
- In the entire bottom 40 percent of the image, across the complete width, use
  only short continuous meadow grass and low non-directional texture. Do not
  place flowers, tall plants, visible stems, broad leaves, shrubs, rocks, paths,
  or distinct objects there. This prevents details that begin below a figure
  from extending upward behind it.
- Build one continuous, level foreground meadow across the full width so all
  three contact points on the yellow baseline share the same depth plane.
- The reserved areas must be visually implicit. Do not draw circles, platforms,
  clearings, spotlight patches, rings, or other visible landing pads around them.
- No path is required. Prefer a natural meadow without a visible path.
- Keep foreground texture subtle directly behind and around the silhouettes.
- Upper-center area must remain calm open sky for deterministic title rendering.
- Middle-center area must remain calm landscape for deterministic set info.
- Keep important scenery away from imaginary card cut lines.

Style:
- Nostalgic 1990s monster-card binder poster background.
- Peaceful countryside starting-town meadow, warm morning light, forest edge,
  distant generic research-lab silhouette, soft mountains, high-detail painterly
  anime adventure background.
- Warm upper-left sunlight, cooler green shadows, rich but print-friendly detail.

Avoid:
- No Pokemon, no creatures, no people, no silhouettes, no props, no signs.
- No readable text, logos, badges, cards, UI, watermark, frames, grid lines,
  dotted guides, crop marks, or borders.
- No three-way path, radial composition, symmetrical lanes, stepping stones,
  any prominent path, pedestals, raised side clearings, or separate ground planes.
- No flowers, tall vegetation, rocks, or high-contrast objects in the bottom
  40 percent, including the page margins.
- Do not imitate an exact copyrighted game location.
